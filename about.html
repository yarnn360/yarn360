Our story

Threads with roots.

YARN360 began with a simple idea — that the clothes we wear every day should feel as natural as the world that inspires them.

The beginning

Born from a love of the natural world.

We started in a small studio in Chennai, drawn to natural fibres, plant dyes, and the quiet craft of making clothes that last. The butterfly and leaves in our mark stand for transformation and growth — the way a good garment becomes part of your story.

Every collection is designed in small batches, so we can be careful about materials, mindful about waste, and honest about how things are made.

What we stand for

Three simple promises

Natural first

Organic and natural fibres, plant-based dyes, low-impact processes.

Made to last

Timeless cuts and honest construction, built to be worn for years.

Small batches

Thoughtful quantities so nothing is wasted and everything is considered.
